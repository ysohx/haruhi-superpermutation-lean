# Authorship and provenance

The new argument, new Lean proof code, computational experiments, proof debugging,
and documentation for this extension were generated by **OpenAI Codex**, including
parallel Codex agents, during an interactive research session.

The human participant proposed the investigation and guided the conversation.
This release makes no claim that the human personally wrote the mathematical
argument or Lean code. No human participant's name, account, or contact information
is included in the repository files.

## Existing work

Codex developed an extension of existing research and formal libraries. The
original contributions and adapted code of Zach Hunter, Uku Raudvere, Xiaolong
Liu, Marin Kisic, and the Lean/mathlib communities remain credited in the
[README](README.md#prior-work-and-attribution). In particular, adapting an upstream
proof does not make that original proof a Codex contribution. Source revisions,
adaptations, and upstream licenses are recorded alongside the proof.

## Verification and review

Codex executed the Lean build and final axiom audit. Other Codex agents also
reviewed the argument and theorem statements. These were AI reviews, not
independent human peer review. The resulting theorem and axiom report are
available for independent reproduction; novelty and publication priority remain
unconfirmed.

This attribution describes how the work was produced. It is not an official
OpenAI research publication or an assertion of OpenAI endorsement.
